Question 1

Motion of a shadow

A person of height 1.6 m walks away from a lamp post of height 4 m along a straight path on level ground. If the person walks at 60 cm s⁻¹, what is the speed of the tip of the person's shadow relative to the person?

Answer: (B) 40 cm s⁻¹

Let x be the person's distance from the lamp post and y be the length of the shadow. By similar triangles, 4/(x+y) = 1.6/y. Hence 4y = 1.6x + 1.6y, so 2.4y = 1.6x and y = (2/3)x.

Differentiating with respect to time gives dy/dt = (2/3) dx/dt = (2/3)(60) = 40 cm s⁻¹. The tip's speed relative to the person equals the rate at which the shadow length increases.

Concept: Similar triangles + relative velocity.

Part B — 2026 Motion in a Plane PYQ

JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 1, Physics question 6. A particle is projected from O at speed v and angle θ and passes through P, 5 m horizontally from O and 1 m above O. Select all correct statements.

Answer and solution

Correct options: A and B. From projectile trajectory y=x tanθ−gx²/(2v²cos²θ), put (x,y)=(5,1). At θ=45°, v=5√g/2. The range is v²/g=25/4=6.25 m, so the apex is at half-range 3.125 m, before P. The official 2026 solution marks A and B.

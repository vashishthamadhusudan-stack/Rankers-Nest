Q1. Car accelerates, then decelerates

1978

A car accelerates from rest at constant rate α for some time and then decelerates at constant rate β until it comes to rest. Total time is t. Find (i) maximum velocity and (ii) total distance travelled.

Answer & solution

vmax = αβt/(α+β); s = αβt²/[2(α+β)]

Let acceleration time be t₁ and retardation time t₂. At the peak speed V, V=αt₁=βt₂ and t₁+t₂=t. Hence V=αβt/(α+β). The v–t graph is a triangle, so s=Vt/2.

Q6. Circular motion: half a period

1983

A particle moves in a circle of radius R. Find (i) displacement magnitude and (ii) distance travelled in half a period.

After half a revolution the particle is at the diametrically opposite point, so displacement magnitude is 2R. Half the circumference is πR.

Q8. Four people chasing around a square

1984

Four people K, L, M, N start at the corners of a square of side d. Each moves with constant speed v directly towards the next person in cyclic order. Find the time when they meet.

Answer & solution

t = d/v

By symmetry the square shrinks self-similarly. Each person's velocity component toward the centre is v/√2, and the initial distance from a corner to the centre is d/√2. Thus t=(d/√2)/(v/√2)=d/v.

Q9. Boat crossing along shortest path

1988

A boat has speed 5 km/h in still water and crosses a river 1 km wide along the shortest possible path in 15 minutes. Find the river velocity.

Answer & solution

B

The across-river ground speed is 1/(15/60)=4 km/h. The boat's 5 km/h speed relative to water has perpendicular components 4 km/h across and u_river upstream. Thus u_river=√(5²−4²)=3 km/h.

Answer & solution

ACD

Q16. Ball thrown inside an accelerating train

2011

Flight time T=2u sin60°/g=√3 s. In the train frame the ball's relative horizontal displacement at return is 5√3−aT²/2. Set this to 1.15 m: a=(5√3−1.15)/1.5≈5.01 m/s², which rounds to 5 m/s² at the given precision.

Q20. Projectile after impact loses half its kinetic energy

2018

A projectile launched at 45° reaches maximum height 120 m. On its first impact it loses half its kinetic energy and immediately after bouncing its velocity makes 30° with the horizontal. Find its next maximum height in metres.

Answer & solution

30

Initial H=u²/(4g)=120, so u²=480g. After losing half the kinetic energy, rebound speed is u/√2. Vertical component is u/(2√2), so H′=(u²/8)/(2g)=u²/(16g)=30 m.

Q24. Shadow-tip speed

2023

A person 1.6 m tall walks away from a 4 m lamp post at 60 cm/s. Find the speed of the tip of the shadow relative to the person, in cm/s.

Answer & solution

40

Let x be the distance of the person from the post and l the shadow length. Similar triangles give 4/(x+l)=1.6/l, hence l=(2/3)x. Therefore dl/dt=(2/3)(60)=40 cm/s.

Q1. A 2 kg body moving at 3 m s⁻¹ collides and sticks to a stationary 1 kg body. Their common speed is:
Correct answer: B. 2 m s⁻¹

Momentum conservation: (2×3 + 1×0)/(2+1) = 2 m s⁻¹.

Q2. The SI unit of linear momentum is:
Correct answer: A. kg m s⁻¹

Momentum p = mv, so its unit is kg m s⁻¹.

Q3. A force of 5 N acts for 0.4 s. The impulse is:
Correct answer: B. 2 N s

Impulse J = FΔt = 5×0.4 = 2 N s.

Q4. In an isolated system, total linear momentum is conserved when:
Correct answer: B. Net external force is zero

Zero net external force means total momentum remains constant.

Q5. A 0.1 kg ball changes velocity from +20 m s⁻¹ to −10 m s⁻¹. Impulse on it is:
Correct answer: B. −1 N s

J = m(vf−vi)=0.1(−10−20)=−3 N s.

Q6. In a perfectly elastic collision, which quantities are conserved for an isolated system?
Correct answer: C. Both momentum and kinetic energy

Momentum and total kinetic energy are conserved in an elastic collision.

Q7. A 4 kg object at rest explodes into 1 kg and 3 kg pieces. If the 1 kg piece moves at 6 m s⁻¹, the other piece moves at:
Correct answer: B. 2 m s⁻¹ opposite direction

Initial momentum is zero: 1×6 + 3v = 0, so v = −2 m s⁻¹.

Q8. A 0.2 kg ball moving at 10 m s⁻¹ rebounds at 5 m s⁻¹ in the opposite direction. Magnitude of momentum change is:
Correct answer: C. 3 kg m s⁻¹

|Δp| = m|vf−vi| = 0.2|−5−10| = 3 kg m s⁻¹.

Modern Physics and Optics

Modern Physics is organized into four separate chapter sections. Optics remains a separate topic group.

1. Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation

Photoelectric effect, Einstein’s photoelectric equation, work function, threshold frequency, stopping potential, de Broglie wavelength and matter waves.

2. Atomic Structure and Nuclei

Bohr model, atomic energy levels and spectra; nuclear size, mass defect, binding energy, radioactivity and nuclear reactions.

3. Semiconductor Devices and Logic Gates

Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, p–n junction diode, rectification, basic electronic devices and logic gates.

4. Electromagnetic Waves (EM Waves)

Displacement current, properties of EM waves, electromagnetic spectrum and common applications.

Optics — separate topic group

Ray Optics and Wave Optics will remain separate from the four Modern Physics sections.

Rankers Connect is building a learning discovery network for students, parents, independent educators and institutes. Create an early educator profile and tell us what you teach.

Apply as an early educator →

Who can join?

  • Independent online and home tutors.
  • JEE, NEET and school subject specialists.
  • Experienced teachers building their own batches.
  • Small coaching institutes and subject-focused academies.

What information should your profile include?

  • Your subjects, classes and exam categories.
  • Teaching experience and qualifications.
  • Online/offline locations and available time slots.
  • Teaching language, batch size and fee range.
  • A short introduction video or sample teaching clip.

Our early-access approach

We intend to prioritise relevant student matching, clear educator profiles and genuine enquiries. Early access does not guarantee a fixed number of leads, admissions or earnings. Profile approval and student matching will be confirmed by the Rankers Connect team.

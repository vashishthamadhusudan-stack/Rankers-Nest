Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Rankers Connect is an early-access learning discovery concept operated by Rankers Nest. This notice explains the intended handling of information submitted through this prototype.

Current prototype status

The enquiry forms on this prototype save information only in the browser's local storage on the device used to submit the form. They do not send the information to Rankers Nest or to a server. Do not enter sensitive personal information or rely on this form to contact us.

Information planned for collection

If and when live enquiry services are activated, we may request a name, phone number, class or exam, subject, preferred learning mode and details needed to connect a learner with an educator. A revised notice and consent flow will be provided before live collection begins.

Contact and requests

A verified business contact channel and data-request process will be published before live lead collection. Until then, this prototype should not be used to submit real student enquiries.

Children

Parents or legal guardians should submit enquiries on behalf of minors. We do not intend to knowingly collect children's personal information through this prototype.

Q1. A 5 kg block rests on a smooth horizontal surface. A horizontal force of 20 N acts on it. Its acceleration is:

Answer: B. 4 m/s²

By Newton’s second law, a = F/m = 20/5 = 4 m/s².

Q2. Two blocks of masses 2 kg and 3 kg are in contact on a frictionless table. A 10 N force pushes the 2 kg block toward the 3 kg block. The contact force is:

Answer: C. 8 N

The system acceleration is 10/(2+3)=2 m/s². The contact force accelerating the 3 kg block is 3×2=6 N.

Q3. A 4 kg block is pulled on a smooth floor by a 20 N force at 60° above the horizontal. Its horizontal acceleration is:

Answer: B. 2.5 m/s²

Horizontal component = 20 cos60° = 10 N; a = 10/4 = 2.5 m/s².

Q4. A 10 kg lift accelerates upward at 2 m/s². Taking g = 10 m/s², the normal reaction on a 60 kg passenger is:

Answer: C. 720 N

For upward acceleration, N−mg=ma; N=m(g+a)=60(12)=720 N.

Q5. A person of mass 50 kg stands in a lift accelerating downward at 2 m/s². For g=10 m/s², the apparent weight is:

Answer: B. 400 N

N=m(g−a)=50(10−2)=400 N.

Q6. A 2 kg body is acted on by two perpendicular forces of 6 N and 8 N. The magnitude of its acceleration is:

Answer: C. 5 m/s²

Resultant force = √(6²+8²)=10 N; acceleration = 10/2=5 m/s².

Q7. A 5 kg block is pulled by 30 N on a horizontal rough surface. If kinetic friction is 10 N, acceleration is:

Answer: B. 4 m/s²

Net force = 30−10=20 N; a=20/5=4 m/s².

Q8. A 2 kg block is pressed against a vertical wall by a horizontal force of 50 N. If μs=0.5 and g=10 m/s², the block can remain at rest because maximum friction is:

Answer: C. 25 N

Normal force is 50 N, so maximum static friction is μN=0.5×50=25 N, greater than weight 20 N.

JEE Main 2021 · 27 August · Evening Shift

Q1. Water drops fall at equal time intervals from a tap 9.8 m above the floor. The first drop reaches the floor exactly when the third drop starts falling. At that instant, how far above the floor is the second drop?

Answer: C. 7.35 m

For the first drop, 9.8 = ½gT², so T = √2 s. The third drop starts at t=T, so each interval is T/2. The second drop has fallen for T/2: s₂ = ½g(T/2)² = h/4 = 2.45 m. Its height above the floor is 9.8 − 2.45 = 7.35 m.

JEE Main 2022 · 29 June · Evening Shift

Q2. A toy starts from rest and moves with constant acceleration. It covers 10 m during the first time interval of duration t. How much distance does it cover during the next interval of the same duration?

Answer: C. 30 m

Starting from rest, 10 = ½at², so at² = 20. Total displacement by 2t is ½a(2t)² = 2at² = 40 m. Distance covered during the second interval = 40 − 10 = 30 m.

JEE Main 2021 · 25 July · Evening Shift

Q3. A balloon rises vertically at a constant 10 m/s. When it is 75 m above the ground, an object is released. Taking g = 10 m/s² and neglecting air resistance, what is the balloon’s height when the object reaches the ground?

Answer: C. 125 m

At release, the object has upward velocity 10 m/s. Taking ground as y=0: 0 = 75 + 10t − 5t², giving t=5 s (positive root). The balloon continues upward at 10 m/s, so its height is 75 + 10×5 = 125 m.

JEE Main 2026 · 28 January · Morning Shift

Q4. Water drops fall from a tap 5 m above the floor at equal time intervals. The first drop hits the floor just as the sixth drop begins to fall. How high above the floor is the fourth drop at that instant? Take g = 9.8 m/s².

Answer: C. 4.2 m

Let T be the first drop's fall time. Five equal intervals fit into T, so the fourth drop has fallen for 2T/5. Since fall distance is proportional to time squared, its fall distance is 5(2/5)² = 0.8 m. Height above floor = 5 − 0.8 = 4.2 m.

JEE Main 2022 · 29 July · Evening Shift

Q5. A ball is released from rest from height h. Let t₁ be the time to fall the first half of the height and t₂ the time for the remaining half. Find t₂/t₁.

Answer: A. √2 − 1

First half: h/2 = ½gt₁², hence t₁=√(h/g). Total time T=√(2h/g)=√2t₁. Thus t₂=T−t₁=(√2−1)t₁, so t₂/t₁=√2−1.

JEE Main 2022 · 29 June · Morning Shift

Q6. Ball A is released from rest from the top of a 180 m tower. Two seconds later, ball B is thrown vertically downward from the same point. They meet 100 m above the ground. Taking g = 10 m/s², find the initial speed of B.

Answer: C. 30 m/s

A falls 80 m, so 80=½(10)t² gives t=4 s. B moves for 2 s. Then 80=2u+½(10)(2²)=2u+20, giving u=30 m/s.

JEE Main 2024 · 9 April · Morning Shift

Q7. A particle travels half its total distance at speed v. It covers the remaining half in two equal time intervals, moving at speeds u and w. Find its average speed.

Answer: B. 2v(u+w)/(u+w+2v)

Let total distance be D. First half time = D/(2v). For the second half, equal time intervals τ at speeds u,w give τ(u+w)=D/2, so total time there is D/(u+w). Average speed = D/[D/(2v)+D/(u+w)] = 2v(u+w)/(u+w+2v).

JEE Main 2024 · 6 April · Evening Shift

Q8. From a tower, a stone projected vertically upward reaches the ground after t₁. With the same speed projected vertically downward, it reaches the ground after t₂. Find the time taken to reach the ground if it is released from rest.

Answer: C. √(t₁t₂)

For upward projection, H=½gt₁²−ut₁. For downward projection, H=ut₂+½gt₂². Equating gives u=½g(t₁−t₂), and substituting yields H=½g t₁t₂. For release from rest, H=½gT², hence T=√(t₁t₂).

JEE Main 2025 · 7 April · Evening Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q9. A helicopter flies horizontally at 360 km/h at an altitude of 2 km and releases an object. Neglecting air resistance and taking g = 10 m/s², find the magnitude of the object's displacement from the release point when it reaches the ground after 20 s.

Answer: B. 2√2 km

Horizontal speed = 360/3.6 = 100 m/s. Horizontal displacement in 20 s = 2000 m. Vertical fall = ½(10)(20²) = 2000 m. Resultant displacement = √(2000² + 2000²) = 2000√2 m = 2√2 km.

JEE Main 2023 · 10 April · Morning Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q10. A projectile is launched at 15° to the horizontal with a speed that gives a horizontal range of 50 m. If it is launched with the same speed at 45°, what is its range? Neglect air resistance.

Answer: C. 100 m

Range R = u²sin2θ/g. At 15°, sin30° = 1/2, so u²/g = 100 m. At 45°, sin90° = 1, giving range 100 m.

JEE Main 2024 · 8 April · Evening Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q11. For a projectile launched from level ground, the horizontal range is numerically equal to its maximum height. Find tan θ, where θ is the angle of projection with the horizontal.

Answer: C. 4

R = u²sin2θ/g and H = u²sin²θ/(2g). Set R = H: 2sinθ cosθ = sin²θ/2, so 4cosθ = sinθ and tanθ = 4.

JEE Main 2024 · 5 April · Shift 2 · Motion in a Plane

Q12. A projectile's initial speed is reduced to half while its projection angle remains unchanged. What is the ratio of the new maximum height H′ to the original maximum height H?

Answer: B. 1/4

Maximum height H = u²sin²θ/(2g). Replacing u by u/2 gives H′ = (u²/4)sin²θ/(2g) = H/4.

JEE Main 2022 · 24 June · Evening Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q13. A body is projected from the ground at 45° to the horizontal. Its speed after 2 s is 20 m/s. Taking g = 10 m/s², find the maximum height reached.

Answer: C. 20 m

Let each initial velocity component be a because θ = 45°. After 2 s, components are a and a − 20. Given speed² = a² + (a−20)² = 400, so a = 20 m/s. Maximum height = a²/(2g) = 400/20 = 20 m.

JEE Main 2025 · 29 January · Morning Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q14. A boat moves downstream at 27 km/h in still water speed, while the river flows at 9 km/h. A ball is thrown vertically upward from the boat at 10 m/s. What horizontal range does the ball have relative to an observer on the river bank? Take g = 10 m/s².

Answer: C. 2000 cm

Horizontal speed relative to the bank = 27 + 9 = 36 km/h = 10 m/s. Time of flight = 2(10)/10 = 2 s. Horizontal range = 10 × 2 = 20 m = 2000 cm.

JEE Main 2020 · 8 January · Morning Slot · Motion in a Plane

Q15. Particle A moves along x with x = 10 + 8t − 3t², while particle B moves along y with y = 5 − 8t³ (SI units). Find the speed of B relative to A at t = 1 s.

Answer: A. 2√145 m/s

vA = dx/dt = 8 − 6t, hence at t=1, vA = 2 m/s along x. vB = dy/dt = −24t², hence at t=1, vB = −24 m/s along y. Relative velocity components are perpendicular, so speed = √(2² + 24²) = √580 = 2√145 m/s.

JEE Main 2024 · 31 January · Morning Shift · Motion in a Straight Line

Q16. A particle's time and position are related by t = mx² + nx, where m and n are constants and v is its instantaneous velocity. Which relation gives its acceleration in terms of v?

Answer: A. a = −2mv³

dt/dx = 2mx+n, so v = dx/dt = 1/(2mx+n). Differentiate v with respect to x: dv/dx = −2m/(2mx+n)² = −2mv². Then a = dv/dt = (dv/dx)(dx/dt) = −2mv³.

JEE Main 2022 · 27 July · Evening Shift · Motion in a Straight Line

Q17. A bullet's speed becomes one-third of its initial speed after penetrating 4 cm into a wooden block. If the resisting force is constant, how much farther will it travel before stopping?

Answer: A. 0.5 cm

With constant deceleration, v² = u² + 2as. After 4 cm, v² = u²/9, so the loss in v² is 8u²/9 over 4 cm. The remaining stopping distance is (u²/9)/(8u²/(9×4)) = 0.5 cm.

JEE Main 2023 · 8 April · Evening Shift · Motion in a Straight Line

Q18. Consider these statements for one-dimensional motion: (I) The area under a velocity–time graph always gives total distance travelled. (II) The area under an acceleration–time graph gives the change in velocity. Which option is correct?

Answer: C. I false, II true

The signed area under a velocity–time graph gives displacement; total distance requires adding the magnitudes of areas. The signed area under an acceleration–time graph gives the change in velocity.

JEE Main 2023 · 31 January · Morning Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q19. A projectile is launched from level ground with initial speed u. At its highest point, its speed is v. Express its total time of flight in terms of u, v and g.

Answer: B. 2√(u²−v²)/g

At the highest point, the vertical velocity is zero, so the horizontal component equals v. The initial vertical component is √(u²−v²). Time of flight = 2u_y/g = 2√(u²−v²)/g.

JEE Main 2026 · 21 January · Evening Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q20. A river is 200 m wide and flows at 18 km/h. A boat moves at 36 km/h in still water. For a round trip across the river, it is steered perpendicular to the banks on each leg. Find total time and downstream displacement.

Answer: B. 40 s, 200 m

Cross-river speed = 36 km/h = 10 m/s, so each 200 m crossing takes 20 s. Current speed = 18 km/h = 5 m/s, giving 100 m drift per leg. Total time is 40 s and total downstream displacement is 200 m.

JEE Main 2025 · 3 April · Evening Shift · Motion in a Plane

Q21. A projectile's horizontal range is three times its maximum height. Find tan θ for its angle of projection θ.

Answer: B. 4/3

R/H = [u²sin2θ/g] / [u²sin²θ/(2g)] = 4cotθ. Given R/H = 3, so 4cotθ = 3 and tanθ = 4/3.

Rankers Nest practice · projectile motion (not a verbatim PYQ)

Q22. A projectile is launched at angle θ to the horizontal. The vertical distance covered in the first second after launch is 20 m, and the vertical distance covered in the last second before reaching maximum height is 5 m. What is the ratio of these distances?

Answer: B. 4:1

The stated distances are 20 m and 5 m, so their ratio is 20:5 = 4:1. This item is a ratio check using the stated interval distances.

Rankers Nest practice · relative velocity (not a verbatim PYQ)

Q23. A river flows east at 3 m/s. A boat moves at 5 m/s relative to still water and is directed so that it crosses straight north with no eastward drift. What is its northward speed relative to the bank?

Answer: C. 4 m/s

The boat's westward component relative to water must be 3 m/s to cancel the current. Its northward component is √(5²−3²) = 4 m/s.

Rankers Nest practice · vector kinematics (not a verbatim PYQ)

Q24. The position of a particle in the xy-plane is r(t) = (3t²)i + (4t)j metres. Find the magnitude of its velocity at t = 2 s.

Answer: D. 4√10 m/s

Velocity = dr/dt = 6t i + 4j. At t = 2 s, components are 12 and 4 m/s, so speed = √(12²+4²) = √160 = 4√10 m/s.

Rankers Nest practice · projectile heights (not a verbatim PYQ)

Q25. Two projectiles are launched with the same speed at angles 30° and 60° to the horizontal. What is the ratio of their maximum heights H₃₀:H₆₀?

Answer: B. 1:3

Maximum height H = u²sin²θ/(2g). Thus H₃₀:H₆₀ = sin²30°:sin²60° = (1/4):(3/4) = 1:3.

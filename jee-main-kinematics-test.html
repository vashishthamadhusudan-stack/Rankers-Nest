MECHANICS · CHAPTER 01

Kinematics — JEE Main PYQ Test

Chapter-only test. These are chapter-classified historical question records with year/shift labels. The wording is paraphrased; answer keys and worked solutions are prepared for Rankers Nest. +4 correct, −1 incorrect, 0 unattempted.

Suggested time: 12 minutes
JEE Main 2021 · 27 August · Evening Shift

Q1. Water drops fall at equal time intervals from a tap 9.8 m above the floor. The first drop reaches the floor exactly when the third drop starts falling. At that instant, how far above the floor is the second drop?

Answer: C. 7.35 m

For the first drop, 9.8 = ½gT², so T = √2 s. The third drop starts at t=T, so each interval is T/2. The second drop has fallen for T/2: s₂ = ½g(T/2)² = h/4 = 2.45 m. Its height above the floor is 9.8 − 2.45 = 7.35 m.

JEE Main 2022 · 29 June · Evening Shift

Q2. A toy starts from rest and moves with constant acceleration. It covers 10 m during the first time interval of duration t. How much distance does it cover during the next interval of the same duration?

Answer: C. 30 m

Starting from rest, 10 = ½at², so at² = 20. Total displacement by 2t is ½a(2t)² = 2at² = 40 m. Distance covered during the second interval = 40 − 10 = 30 m.

JEE Main 2021 · 25 July · Evening Shift

Q3. A balloon rises vertically at a constant 10 m/s. When it is 75 m above the ground, an object is released. Taking g = 10 m/s² and neglecting air resistance, what is the balloon’s height when the object reaches the ground?

Answer: C. 125 m

At release, the object has upward velocity 10 m/s. Taking ground as y=0: 0 = 75 + 10t − 5t², giving t=5 s (positive root). The balloon continues upward at 10 m/s, so its height is 75 + 10×5 = 125 m.

JEE Main 2026 · 28 January · Morning Shift

Q4. Water drops fall from a tap 5 m above the floor at equal time intervals. The first drop hits the floor just as the sixth drop begins to fall. How high above the floor is the fourth drop at that instant? Take g = 9.8 m/s².

Answer: C. 4.2 m

Let T be the first drop's fall time. Five equal intervals fit into T, so the fourth drop has fallen for 2T/5. Since fall distance is proportional to time squared, its fall distance is 5(2/5)² = 0.8 m. Height above floor = 5 − 0.8 = 4.2 m.

JEE Main 2022 · 29 July · Evening Shift

Q5. A ball is released from rest from height h. Let t₁ be the time to fall the first half of the height and t₂ the time for the remaining half. Find t₂/t₁.

Answer: A. √2 − 1

First half: h/2 = ½gt₁², hence t₁=√(h/g). Total time T=√(2h/g)=√2t₁. Thus t₂=T−t₁=(√2−1)t₁, so t₂/t₁=√2−1.

JEE Main 2022 · 29 June · Morning Shift

Q6. Ball A is released from rest from the top of a 180 m tower. Two seconds later, ball B is thrown vertically downward from the same point. They meet 100 m above the ground. Taking g = 10 m/s², find the initial speed of B.

Answer: C. 30 m/s

A falls 80 m, so 80=½(10)t² gives t=4 s. B moves for 2 s. Then 80=2u+½(10)(2²)=2u+20, giving u=30 m/s.

JEE Main 2024 · 9 April · Morning Shift

Q7. A particle travels half its total distance at speed v. It covers the remaining half in two equal time intervals, moving at speeds u and w. Find its average speed.

Answer: B. 2v(u+w)/(u+w+2v)

Let total distance be D. First half time = D/(2v). For the second half, equal time intervals τ at speeds u,w give τ(u+w)=D/2, so total time there is D/(u+w). Average speed = D/[D/(2v)+D/(u+w)] = 2v(u+w)/(u+w+2v).

JEE Main 2024 · 6 April · Evening Shift

Q8. From a tower, a stone projected vertically upward reaches the ground after t₁. With the same speed projected vertically downward, it reaches the ground after t₂. Find the time taken to reach the ground if it is released from rest.

Answer: C. √(t₁t₂)

For upward projection, H=½gt₁²−ut₁. For downward projection, H=ut₂+½gt₂². Equating gives u=½g(t₁−t₂), and substituting yields H=½g t₁t₂. For release from rest, H=½gT², hence T=√(t₁t₂).

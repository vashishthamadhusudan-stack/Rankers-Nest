Q1. A solid disc and a ring have equal mass and radius. Their moments of inertia about the central axis are respectively:
Correct answer: B. ½MR² and MR²

For a solid disc I = ½MR²; for a thin ring I = MR².

Q2. A torque of 6 N m acts on a body with moment of inertia 3 kg m². Angular acceleration is:
Correct answer: B. 2 rad s⁻²

Using τ = Iα, α = 6/3 = 2 rad s⁻².

Q3. A wheel starts from rest with constant angular acceleration 4 rad s⁻². Its angular speed after 3 s is:
Correct answer: B. 12 rad s⁻¹

ω = ω₀ + αt = 0 + 4×3 = 12 rad s⁻¹.

Q4. A particle of mass m moves in a circle of radius r with speed v. Its angular momentum magnitude about the centre is:
Correct answer: B. mvr

L = r × p, so magnitude L = mvr.

Q5. If the moment of inertia of a rotating body is halved while angular momentum stays constant, angular speed becomes:
Correct answer: C. Double

L = Iω is constant; halving I doubles ω.

Q6. A uniform rod of mass M and length L has moment of inertia about an axis through its centre perpendicular to the rod:
Correct answer: B. ML²/12

For a uniform rod about its centre, I = ML²/12.

Q7. A body rolls without slipping. The relation between centre speed v and angular speed ω for radius R is:
Correct answer: B. v = ωR

Pure rolling gives v_cm = ωR.

Q8. A torque of 10 N m rotates a body through 2 rad in the direction of the torque. Work done is:
Correct answer: C. 20 J

For constant torque, W = τθ = 10×2 = 20 J.

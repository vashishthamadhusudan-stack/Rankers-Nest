## Scope and limitation

- This is a focused chapter test, not an exhaustive catalogue of every Newton's Laws question from every JEE Advanced year.
- The site labels historical questions by year/paper/question number and labels original questions as practice; practice is not misrepresented as a PYQ.
- Historical question stems are paraphrased and are not facsimile reproductions. The official PDFs remain the authoritative source for original figures and complete wording.

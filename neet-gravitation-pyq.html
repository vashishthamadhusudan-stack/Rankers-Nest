Q1. If the distance between two point masses is doubled, the gravitational force becomes:
Correct answer: C. One-fourth

F ∝ 1/r²; doubling r reduces force to one-fourth.

Q2. The value of acceleration due to gravity at height h above Earth is approximately g(1−2h/R) when:
Correct answer: B. h ≪ R

For small heights h compared with Earth radius R, g_h ≈ g(1−2h/R).

Q3. The escape speed from a planet of mass M and radius R is:
Correct answer: B. √(2GM/R)

Escape speed follows from energy conservation: v_e = √(2GM/R).

Q4. A satellite in a circular orbit has orbital speed:
Correct answer: A. √(GM/r)

Equating gravitational and centripetal forces gives v = √(GM/r).

Q5. The gravitational potential at distance r from a point mass M, taking zero at infinity, is:
Correct answer: B. −GM/r

Gravitational potential V = −GM/r.

Q6. If Earth’s mass stayed constant but its radius became half, surface gravity would be:
Correct answer: D. 4g

g = GM/R²; halving R makes g four times larger.

Q7. Kepler’s third law for planets around the same star states:
Correct answer: B. T² ∝ r³

The square of orbital period is proportional to the cube of orbital radius/semimajor axis.

Q8. The total mechanical energy of a satellite in a circular orbit of radius r is:
Correct answer: C. −GMm/(2r)

K = GMm/(2r), U = −GMm/r, so E = −GMm/(2r).

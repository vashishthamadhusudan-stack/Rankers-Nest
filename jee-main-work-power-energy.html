JEE Main PYQ · 2026 · 22 Jan · Shift 2

Q7. Statement I: For a conservative force, the work done is W = −∫ F·dr. Statement II: For a conservative force, work depends on the path. Choose the correct statement combination.

Options:
  1. Both statements I and II are false
  2. Statement I is false but Statement II is true
  3. Statement I is true but Statement II is false
  4. Both statements I and II are true

Correct answer: A — both statements are false

The work done by the force is ∫F·dr; work by a conservative force is path-independent.

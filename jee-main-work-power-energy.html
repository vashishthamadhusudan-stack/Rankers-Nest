Q1. A raindrop of mass 1 g starts from rest at a height of 1 km and reaches the ground with speed 5 m s⁻¹. Find the work done by the unknown resistive force. (g = 10 m s⁻²)

Correct answer: D. −9.98 J

JEE Main 2026 · 6 April · Shift 1

Working: Work–energy theorem: W_g + W_r = ΔK. Here W_g = mgh = 10 J and ΔK = ½mv² = 0.0125 J, so W_r = 0.0125 − 10 ≈ −9.99 J.

Q2. A body of mass 1 kg moves along a straight line with velocity v = 2x². Find the work done by the body during displacement from x = 0 to 5 m.

Correct answer: C. 1250 J

JEE Main 2026 · 6 April · Shift 2

Working: Work–energy theorem gives W = ½m(v²−u²). Since v = 2x², at x = 5 m the speed is 50 m/s and the initial speed at x = 0 is zero. Hence W = ½(1)(50²) = 1250 J.

Correct answer: D. mα²d/2

Correct answer: A. v_B : v_A

Working: Kinetic energy is proportional to speed squared. The fractional loss is 1 − (40²/100²) = 0.84, i.e. 84%.

Q22. A 50 g bullet is fired at 100 m/s into plywood and emerges at 40 m/s. The percentage loss of kinetic energy is:

Correct answer: A. 84%

Working: Constant power gives P = mv(dv/dt) = constant, so v² ∝ t and v ∝ t^(1/2). Integrating v with respect to time gives displacement x ∝ t^(3/2).

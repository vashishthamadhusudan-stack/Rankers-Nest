Q1. A raindrop of mass 1 g starts from rest at a height of 1 km and reaches the ground with speed 5 m s⁻¹. Find the work done by the unknown resistive force. (g = 10 m s⁻²)

Correct answer: D. −9.99 J JEE Main 2026 · 6 April · Shift 1

Working: Work–energy theorem gives Wg + Wr = ΔK. Here Wg=mgh=10 J and ΔK=½mv²=0.0125 J, so Wr=0.0125−10=−9.9875 J≈−9.99 J.

Q2. A body of mass 1 kg moves along a straight line with velocity v = 2x². Find the work done by the body during displacement from x = 0 to 5 m.

Correct answer: C. 1250 J

JEE Main 2026 · 6 April · Shift 2

Working: Work–energy theorem gives W = ½m(v²−u²). Since v = 2x², at x = 5 m the speed is 50 m/s and the initial speed at x = 0 is zero. Hence W = ½(1)(50²) = 1250 J.

Q3. A 1 kg block is acted on by forces (2i + 3j + 4k) N and (3i − j − 2k) N. It moves 25 m in the direction (3i − 4j). Find the net work done.

Correct answer: A. 35 J

Net force is (5i+2j+2k) N. The displacement unit vector is (3i−4j)/5, so W=F·s=25[(5)(3/5)+(2)(−4/5)]=25(7/5)=35 J.

Q4. A 2 kg body starts from rest under the force F=(2t i + 6t² j) N. Find the instantaneous power at t=2 s.

Correct answer: C. 200 W

a=F/m=(t i+3t² j) m s⁻². Integrating from rest gives v=(t²/2)i+t³j. At t=2 s, v=2i+8j and F=4i+24j, so P=F·v=8+192=200 W.

Q5. A 2 kg spherical ball falls from a height of 10 m and comes to rest after penetrating 10 cm into sand. Find the average force exerted by sand on the ball. (g = 10 m s⁻²)

Correct answer: B. 2020 N JEE Main 2026 · 2 April · Shift 2

Working: Impact kinetic energy is mgh=2×10×10=200 J. Over the 0.10 m penetration, gravity does +2 J and the sand force F does −0.10F. Since ΔK=−200 J, −0.10F+2=−200, so F=2020 N upward.

Q11. A 25 kg block is pulled uniformly through 5 m on a horizontal surface by a force at 45° above the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.25. Taking g=9.8 m s⁻², find the work done by the applied force.

Correct answer: C. 245 J

Uniform motion gives F cos45°=μN and N=mg−F sin45°. Thus F(cos45°+μ sin45°)=μmg. Work Fs cos45°=μmgs/(1+μ)=0.25×25×9.8×5/1.25=245 J.

Q12. A particle is released from height S above Earth's surface. At a certain height its kinetic energy is three times its potential energy. The height above the surface and speed at that instant are respectively:

Correct answer: D. S/4, √(3gS/2)

Working: At height h, U=mgh and K=mg(S−h). Given K=3U gives S−h=3h, so h=S/4. Then ½mv²=mg(S−h)=3mgS/4, hence v=√(3gS/2).

Q13. A 1 kg block moving at 10 m/s enters a rough region from x = 0.1 m to x = 1.9 m. The retarding force is Fᵣ = −kx, with k = 10 N/m. Find its final speed.

Correct answer: D. 8 m/s

Q14. A conveyor belt moves at speed v. Sand falls onto it from rest at a mass rate λ, where λ is proportional to √v. Neglecting other losses, the power required to bring the sand up to belt speed is proportional to:

Correct answer: B. v^(5/2)

Each unit mass gains kinetic energy v²/2, so P=(1/2)λv². Since λ∝√v, P∝v^(5/2).

Q15. A 4 kg body moves from P(3,4) m to Q(6,10) m under F = (2i + 3j) N in 4 s. The ratio of average power to instantaneous power at t = 4 s is:

Correct answer: D. 12:25

Working: Δr=(3i+6j) m, so W=F·Δr=2×3+3×6=24 J and average power is 6 W. Acceleration is F/m=(0.5i+0.75j) m/s². From Δr=v₀t+½at² over 4 s, v₀=−0.25i m/s and v(4)=1.75i+3j m/s. Instantaneous power at 4 s is F·v=2(1.75)+3(3)=12.5 W. Ratio 6:12.5=12:25.

Q20. A particle of mass m moves in a straight line with velocity v = α√x. Find the total work done by all forces during displacement from x = 0 to x = d.

Correct answer: D. mα²d/2

Working: By work–energy theorem, Wnet=ΔK=½m(vf²−vi²). With v=α√x, vi=0 at x=0 and vf²=α²d, so Wnet=mα²d/2.

Q21. A stationary particle breaks into two parts of masses m_A and m_B with velocities v_A and v_B. The ratio of kinetic energies K_B : K_A is:

Correct answer: A. vB : vA

Working: Since initial momentum is zero, mAvA=mBvB. Therefore KB/KA=(½mBvB²)/(½mAvA²)=vB/vA.

Q22. A 50 g bullet is fired at 100 m/s into plywood and emerges at 40 m/s. The percentage loss of kinetic energy is:

Correct answer: A. 84%

Working: Percentage loss=[1−Kf/Ki]×100=[1−(vf/vi)²]×100=[1−(40/100)²]×100=84%.

Correct answer: C. t^(3/2)

Q24. A 50 kg body is lifted vertically through 20 m by two different methods. Neglecting air resistance, what is the ratio of work done against gravity by the two methods?

Correct answer: A. 1:1

Work against gravity depends only on height change, W=mgΔh. Both methods raise the same body through the same height, so the ratio is 1:1.

Q25. A rubber ball is dropped from height h and rebounds to height h/2. What percentage of its mechanical energy is lost during the collision with the ground?

Correct answer: B. 50%

Before impact the mechanical energy is mgh; after rebound it is mg(h/2). Fractional loss=[mgh−mg(h/2)]/(mgh)=1/2=50%.

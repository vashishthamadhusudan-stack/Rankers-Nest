Q1. A car negotiates a banked curve of radius 300 m at a banking angle of 30°. If the coefficient of static friction is 0.2, estimate the maximum safe speed. Take g = 10 m s⁻² and √3 = 1.73.

Answer: B. 51.4 m s⁻¹

At maximum speed friction acts down the bank. v²/r = g(tanθ + μ)/(1 − μ tanθ). With tan30° = 1/√3, v ≈ 51.4 m s⁻¹.

JEE Main · 2024 · 1 February · Morning Shift

Q2. A 0.5 kg ball is rotated in a horizontal circle of radius 0.5 m. The string can withstand a maximum tension of 400 N. Find the maximum angular speed.

Answer: B. 40 rad s⁻¹

T = mω²r. Therefore ω = √(T/mr) = √(400/(0.5×0.5)) = 40 rad s⁻¹.

JEE Main · 2024 · 31 January · Morning Shift

Q3. A coin is placed on a horizontal rotating disc. The coefficient of friction between the coin and disc is μ, and the coin is at radius r. What is the maximum angular speed before slipping?

Answer: A. √(μg/r)

Static friction provides centripetal force: mω²r ≤ μmg. Thus ωmax = √(μg/r).

JEE Main · 2024 · 29 January · Morning Shift

Q4. Two particles of equal mass move in circles of radii r₁ and r₂. If their centripetal forces are equal, what is the ratio of their speeds v₁/v₂?

Answer: B. √(r₁/r₂)

For equal masses and equal centripetal force, mv₁²/r₁ = mv₂²/r₂. Hence v₁/v₂ = √(r₁/r₂).

Q7. A stone of mass m is tied to a string and moves in a vertical circle of radius r. At the lowest point its speed is v. What is the tension there?

Answer: C. m(v²/r + g)

At the lowest point, T − mg = mv²/r. Therefore T = m(v²/r + g).

JEE Main · 2023 · 13 April · Evening Shift

Q8. A vehicle of mass m moves on a level circular road of radius r with angular speed ω. Find the centripetal force.

Answer: A. mrω²

The linear speed is v = rω. Hence Fc = mv²/r = m(rω)²/r = mrω².

JEE Main · 2023 · 11 April · Morning Shift

Q9. A coin on a rotating table is just about to slip at radius r when the table’s angular speed is ω. If the angular speed is reduced to ω/2, at what radius will it be just about to slip?

Answer: D. 4r

At the limiting condition μmg = mω²r. The limiting radius varies as 1/ω². Halving ω makes the limiting radius four times as large: 4r.

JEE Main · 2023 · 6 April · Evening Shift

Q10. A particle moves at constant speed v on a circular path of radius R from A to B along a quarter-circle. Find the magnitude of its average velocity over this motion.

Answer: B. 2√2v/π

The chord displacement is R√2. Time for a quarter circle is (πR/2)/v. Average-velocity magnitude = R√2 ÷ (πR/2v) = 2√2v/π.

Q11. A child of mass m moves in a horizontal circle of radius r on a merry-go-round that completes one revolution in time T. What is the magnitude of the centripetal force?

Answer: A. 4π²mr/T²

ω = 2π/T, so Fc = mrω² = 4π²mr/T².

Q13. A car moves on a circular track of radius 600 m. Its tangential acceleration equals its centripetal acceleration at every instant. Starting at 54 km h⁻¹, find the time to complete the first quarter of the circle.

Answer: B. 31.7 s

Tangential acceleration aₜ=dv/dt equals centripetal acceleration v²/r, so dv/dt=v²/r. With v₀=15 m/s and r=600 m, integrating gives arc length s=−r ln(1−v₀t/r). For a quarter circle, s=πr/2, hence t=(r/v₀)(1−e^(−π/2))≈31.7 s.

Q15. A car can negotiate a level circular road of radius 75 m at a maximum speed of 30 m s⁻¹ without skidding. If the radius is changed to 48 m and the road/tyres are otherwise unchanged, find the new maximum speed.

Answer: C. 24 m s⁻¹

On a level road vmax = √(μrg), so speed scales as √r. v₂ = 30√(48/75) = 24 m s⁻¹.

JEE Main · 2021 · 25 February · Morning Shift

Q16. A bob on a string of length 1 m moves in a vertical circle. The maximum and minimum tensions are in the ratio 5:1. Find the speed at the highest point. Take g = 10 m s⁻².

Answer: B. 5 m s⁻¹

At top Ttop = mv²/r − mg; at bottom Tbottom = m(v²+4gr)/r + mg. Using Tbottom/Ttop = 5 and r = 1 m gives v² = 25, hence v = 5 m s⁻¹.

Q17. A mass attached to a string of length l makes n rotations per second about a vertical axis through the other end, as in the original paper. The question asks for the string tension in terms of the given mass, length and rotation rate.

Answer: A. 4π²mn²l

Angular speed ω = 2πn. The string tension provides centripetal force: T = mω²l = 4π²mn²l.

Q18. A tube of length 1 m contains an ideal liquid of mass 2M and is closed at both ends. It rotates uniformly in a horizontal plane about one end. The force on the far end is F. The original question asks for the angular speed in terms of the stated quantities.

Answer: A. √(F/M)

For a uniform liquid of mass 2M over length L=1 m, the force at the far end is F=∫₀ᴸ(2M/L)ω²r dr=Mω²L=Mω². Therefore ω=√(F/M), option A.

Q21. A body moves uniformly in a circle of radius R with speed v. Which expression gives its centripetal acceleration?

Answer: B. v²/R

For uniform circular motion, ac = v²/R directed toward the centre.

Q22. A particle completes one revolution of a circle of radius R in time T. What is its angular speed?

Answer: B. 2π/T

Angular displacement in one revolution is 2π radians, so ω = 2π/T.

Practice question · Not a verified PYQ

Q23. A vehicle moves around a level circular track. Which force supplies the required centripetal force when the tyres do not slip?

Answer: C. Static friction

On a level road, static friction between tyres and road provides the centripetal force.

Practice question · Not a verified PYQ

Q24. A particle moves with constant speed in a circle of radius R. What is the angle between its velocity and acceleration at any instant?

Answer: C. 90°

Velocity is tangential and centripetal acceleration is radial, so they are perpendicular.

Practice question · Not a verified PYQ

Q25. A particle moves uniformly in a circle of radius R with period T. Find the distance it travels in half a revolution.

Answer: A. πR

Half a revolution covers half the circumference: distance = (1/2)(2πR) = πR.

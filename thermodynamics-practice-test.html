2. Calorimetry
Heat exchange, latent heat, thermal equilibrium and mixture problems.
3. Specific Heat & Heat Capacity
Specific heat, molar heat capacities, Cₚ, Cᵥ and calorimetric calculations.
4. Laws of Thermodynamics
First law, thermodynamic processes, work, internal energy, heat engines and refrigerators.
5. Heat Transfer
Conduction, composite rods, insulation, radiation and Newton’s law of cooling.

# Rankers Nest — Official PYQ Source Register

## Authoritative source portals

- JEE Main NTA Documents: https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/documents/
- JEE Main NTA current notices and answer-key documents: https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document-category/current-events/
- JEE Main 2025 public notices and final-key material: https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document-category/2025-public-notices/
- NEET UG NTA Documents: https://neet.nta.nic.in/documents/
- NEET UG 2025 public notices and final answer key: https://neet.nta.nic.in/document-category/neetug-2025-public-notices/
- NEET UG 2026 notices and final answer-key material: https://neet.nta.nic.in/public-notices/

## Required evidence per question

A question is not marked source-verified from a year label alone. The reviewer must record:
1. Exam (JEE Main or NEET/AIPMT), year, session/date/shift, and question number or question ID when available.
2. The original question statement and all options, including diagram/graph/circuit orientation and units.
3. Official final answer key. If NTA changed or dropped a question, record that resolution rather than preserving an obsolete key.
4. Independent calculation and a stepwise solution that leads to the keyed option.
5. A check that the displayed option text matches the answer letter/index used by the website scoring code.
6. Status and evidence link. Use Verified, Corrected, Unverified, or Needs Original Figure.

## Important limits

The official NTA portals publish notices and final answer-key documents, but a final answer-key PDF by itself may not contain the full question statement or figure. If the original paper/recorded response/figure cannot be accessed, the question cannot be certified as an exact PYQ. It must be labelled as practice or held from a PYQ-only test until verified.

## Current audit policy

The site-wide inventory and Chromium checks are generated by `scripts/website-pyq-audit.cjs`, `scripts/website-browser-audit.cjs`, and `scripts/quiz-browser-qa.cjs`. Automated checks are quality gates, not substitutes for source-by-source academic verification. See the GitHub Actions workflow named **Website and PYQ Quality Audit** for run reports.

1. Theory notes you can remember

Heat, temperature and internal energy

  • Temperature indicates the thermal state of a system; heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference.
  • Internal energy (U) is the microscopic energy stored in the system. For an ideal gas, it depends only on temperature.
  • Work done by the gas is positive during expansion and negative during compression.
First law: Q = ΔU + W
Q = heat supplied to the system; ΔU = change in internal energy; W = work done by the system.

Equivalent form: ΔU = Q − W. Use this form when the question gives heat absorbed and work done by the gas.

2. The four processes

Isochoric (constant volume): W = 0, so Q = ΔU.
Isothermal (ideal gas, constant temperature): ΔU = 0, so Q = W.
Adiabatic: Q = 0, so ΔU = −W.
Cyclic process: ΔU over one complete cycle = 0, so net Q = net W.

PV-diagram memory: work done by a gas is the signed area under the process curve. Expansion generally gives W > 0; compression gives W < 0.

3. Worked example

An ideal gas absorbs 500 J of heat and does 180 J of work on its surroundings. Find its change in internal energy.

ΔU = Q − W = 500 − 180 = 320 J

Check the signs: heat absorbed is positive; work done by the gas is also positive. The remaining energy increases internal energy.

5. Answer key & explanations

Reveal solutions
1. C — 400 J. 100 J of work is done on the gas, so work done by the gas W = −100 J. ΔU = Q − W = 300 − (−100) = 400 J.
2. C — Zero. Volume is constant, so W = ∫P dV = 0.
3. B — Change in internal energy. For an ideal gas, internal energy depends only on temperature; isothermal means ΔT = 0, hence ΔU = 0.
4. B — Decreases. Adiabatic means Q = 0. Thus ΔU = −W; positive work by the gas gives negative ΔU.
5. C — Zero. Internal energy is a state function; after a full cycle the system returns to its initial state.

6. 60-second recap

  • First law: ΔU = Q − W when W means work done by the system.
  • Isochoric: W = 0.
  • Isothermal ideal gas: ΔU = 0.
  • Adiabatic: Q = 0.
  • Cycle: ΔU = 0.

Memory hook: “V stays → Work Zero; T stays → U stays (ideal gas); Q absent → Adiabatic; Cycle closes → ΔU closes.”

21 October 2026 · Day 3 — Specific Heat Capacities of Gases: Cp, Cv, Mayer's relation and γ. This archived lesson preserves today's theory, worked example, MCQs and explanations.

20-minute studyOriginal Rankers Nest content5 MCQs

1. Quick theory notes

  • Cv is molar heat capacity at constant volume; Cp is molar heat capacity at constant pressure.
  • For an ideal gas, Cp − Cv = R and γ = Cp/Cv.
  • Monatomic: Cv = 3R/2, Cp = 5R/2, γ = 5/3.
  • Rigid diatomic gas at ordinary temperatures: Cv = 5R/2, Cp = 7R/2, γ = 7/5.
Q = nCvΔT (constant volume); Q = nCpΔT (constant pressure)

2. Worked example

For one mole of an ideal gas with Cv = 3R, Mayer's relation gives Cp = 4R, so γ = Cp/Cv = 4/3.

DAY 1 · CURRENT LESSON

Heat & Thermodynamics

Today’s lesson is published as the first step. The roadmap will progress systematically, with completed subtopics marked only after their lessons are checked and published. The checklist below is the master syllabus queue; it is not a claim that every topic has already been taught.

1. Units, Measurements & Mathematical Tools

  • Physical quantities, SI units, dimensions and dimensional analysis
  • Significant figures, errors, uncertainty and measurement
  • Scalars, vectors, components, dot product and cross product
  • Graphs, slopes, areas, basic calculus used in Physics

2. Mechanics

  • Motion in one and two dimensions; position, displacement, velocity and acceleration
  • Projectile motion, relative motion and uniform circular motion
  • Newton’s laws, free-body diagrams, normal force, tension, pulleys and friction
  • Work, energy, power, conservative forces and potential-energy curves
  • Centre of mass, linear momentum, impulse, conservation laws and collisions
  • Rotational kinematics, torque, angular momentum, moment of inertia, rolling and rotational energy
  • Gravitation, field and potential, satellites, escape speed and Kepler’s laws
  • Elasticity, stress-strain, pressure, buoyancy, fluid flow, continuity, Bernoulli, viscosity and surface tension
  • Simple harmonic motion, springs, pendulum, energy in SHM and damped/forced motion where applicable
  • Waves, sound, superposition, standing waves, strings, pipes, beats and Doppler effect

3. Thermal Physics

  • Temperature, thermal expansion, calorimetry, phase change and heat transfer
  • Kinetic theory of gases, ideal gas equation, degrees of freedom and specific heats
  • Thermodynamic systems, state variables, heat, work and internal energy
  • First law; isothermal, isobaric, isochoric, adiabatic and cyclic processes
  • PV diagrams, heat engines, refrigerators, second law and efficiency/COP

4. Electrostatics — detailed sequence

  • Electric charge, conservation/quantisation of charge and Coulomb’s law
  • Superposition; force due to multiple point charges and equilibrium cases
  • Electric field, field lines, continuous charge distributions and electric dipole
  • Electric flux and Gauss’s law; choosing Gaussian surfaces using symmetry
  • Field due to line, plane sheet, conducting sphere, uniformly charged solid sphere, spherical shell and hollow sphere — inside, surface and outside cases
  • Electric potential and potential difference; potential due to point charges, dipoles and distributions
  • Relation between electric field and potential, equipotential surfaces and potential-energy changes
  • Conductors in electrostatic equilibrium, shielding and charge distribution
  • Capacitance, parallel-plate capacitor, dielectric, series/parallel combinations, energy and energy density
  • Dielectric insertion with battery connected/disconnected; force/energy changes and standard capacitor networks

5. Current Electricity

  • Electric current, drift velocity, mobility and current density
  • Ohm’s law, resistance, resistivity, conductivity and temperature dependence
  • Series-parallel networks, cells, EMF, internal resistance and terminal voltage
  • Kirchhoff’s laws and multi-loop circuits
  • Wheatstone bridge, meter bridge and potentiometer
  • Electrical power, heating effect and practical circuit reasoning

6. Moving Charges & Magnetism

  • Lorentz force, magnetic force on a wire and charged-particle motion in fields
  • Velocity selector, helical motion and cyclotron
  • Biot–Savart law and fields due to a wire, circular loop and arc
  • Ampere’s law; long wire, solenoid and toroid
  • Force between parallel currents, torque on a loop and magnetic dipole moment

7. Magnetism & Matter

  • Bar magnet as a dipole, field lines and axial/equatorial fields
  • Magnetic dipole in a field, magnetisation and magnetic materials
  • Dia-, para- and ferromagnetism; hysteresis and Earth’s magnetism where prescribed

8. Electromagnetic Induction & Alternating Current

  • Magnetic flux, Faraday’s law, Lenz’s law and motional EMF
  • Self/mutual inductance, inductors, stored magnetic energy and eddy currents
  • AC voltage/current, RMS and peak values, reactance, impedance and phasors
  • Series LCR circuits, resonance, power factor, transformers and generators

9. Electromagnetic Waves

  • Displacement current, properties and transverse nature of EM waves
  • Electromagnetic spectrum, order, sources and applications

10. Optics

  • Reflection/refraction, mirrors, lenses, lens maker’s formula and combinations
  • Prism, total internal reflection, optical instruments and ray diagrams
  • Wavefronts, Huygens’ principle, interference, Young’s double slit experiment
  • Diffraction, polarisation and resolving power where prescribed

11. Modern Physics & Electronics

  • Photoelectric effect, Einstein equation, matter waves and de Broglie wavelength
  • Atomic models, spectra, Bohr model and energy levels
  • Nuclear size, mass defect, binding energy, radioactivity, fission and fusion
  • Semiconductors, intrinsic/extrinsic materials, p-n junction, diodes, rectifiers and logic gates as prescribed
  • Experimental skills, graphs, instruments and remaining exam-specific syllabus items

How progress is recorded

Daily lessons will follow this roadmap in a sensible sequence, splitting broad headings into separate focused lessons where needed. JEE Advanced depth, JEE Main scope and NEET emphasis will be distinguished. This checklist is a master plan; daily updates will record the exact subtopic completed and what comes next.

A simple study routine

How to use these resources

Choose one chapter, solve questions without looking at the answer, review every mistake, and return to the same concept after revision. Track accuracy as well as score; a wrong answer can reveal the next concept to work on.

Are these resources for JEE and NEET?

The site includes separate JEE and NEET pages. Choose the exam and chapter that match your current preparation.

Where should I begin?

Start with the PYQ Solutions page or choose a chapter card above. If you are unsure about your weak areas, try the Physics Weakness Scanner.

How often should I practise?

Short, regular sessions followed by error analysis are more useful than attempting many questions without reviewing them.

Where can I learn about classes?

Visit the Online Physics Classes page for available learning options and further information.

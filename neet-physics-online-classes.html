How to prepare NEET Physics consistently

Understand each formula

Learn what each quantity means, its unit, sign convention and conditions of use before memorising a relation.

Practise topic-wise MCQs

Start with questions from the topic you have studied, then mix topics to improve recognition of the underlying principle.

Track accuracy

Record incorrect and guessed answers, identify the reason, and revise the concept before attempting a fresh set.

Revise in short cycles

Return to important formulas, diagrams and previous mistakes regularly rather than postponing all revision until the end.

Kinematics

Motion graphs, projectile motion and relative motion

Q1. A particle starts from rest and moves with constant acceleration 2 m/s² for 5 s. Its displacement is:

Answer: C. 25 m

s = ½at² = ½ × 2 × 25 = 25 m.

Q2. A body moves with initial velocity 4 m/s and acceleration 3 m/s² for 2 s. Final velocity is:

Answer: C. 10 m/s

v = u + at = 4 + 3×2 = 10 m/s.

Q3. A projectile is launched at 20 m/s at 30° to the horizontal. Take g = 10 m/s². Time of flight is:

Answer: B. 2 s

T = 2u sinθ/g = 2×20×½/10 = 2 s.

CHAPTER 02

Newton’s Laws of Motion

Free-body diagrams, connected bodies and Newton’s laws

Browse chapter-wise PYQs ↗

Q1. A net force of 12 N acts on a 3 kg body. Its acceleration is:

Answer: C. 4 m/s²

Newton’s second law: a = F/m = 12/3 = 4 m/s².

Q2. A 2 kg block is pulled on a smooth horizontal surface by 10 N. Acceleration is:

Answer: B. 5 m/s²

a = F/m = 10/2 = 5 m/s².

Q3. A person of mass 60 kg stands in a lift accelerating upward at 2 m/s². Take g=10 m/s². Normal reaction is:

Answer: C. 720 N

N = m(g+a) = 60(10+2) = 720 N.

CHAPTER 03

Circular Motion

Centripetal acceleration, banking and vertical circles

Q1. A 2 kg body moves at 4 m/s in a circle of radius 2 m. Centripetal force is:

Answer: C. 16 N

F = mv²/r = 2×16/2 = 16 N.

Q2. A particle moves in a circle of radius r with speed v. If speed doubles and radius stays fixed, centripetal acceleration becomes:

Answer: B. 4 times

a_c = v²/r, so doubling v makes acceleration four times.

Q3. For a vehicle on a level circular road, the centripetal force is provided mainly by:

Answer: C. friction

Static friction between tyres and road supplies the inward force.

CHAPTER 04

Friction

Limiting friction, inclined planes and connected blocks

Browse chapter-wise PYQs ↗

The 3 questions in this mini-test are Rankers Nest original practice questions, not verbatim historical PYQs. Use the source link for the chapter-wise historical question collection.

Q1. A 5 kg block rests on a horizontal surface with μs=0.4. Take g=10 m/s². Maximum static friction is:

Answer: C. 20 N

f_max = μsN = 0.4×5×10 = 20 N.

Q2. A block slides on a horizontal surface with μk=0.2. If its normal reaction is 50 N, kinetic friction is:

Answer: B. 10 N

f_k = μ_k N = 0.2×50 = 10 N.

Q3. The angle of repose θ for a surface with coefficient of limiting friction μ satisfies:

Answer: C. tanθ=μ

At limiting equilibrium, mg sinθ = μmg cosθ, hence tanθ=μ.

CHAPTER 05

Work, Power and Energy

Work-energy theorem, power and potential energy

Browse chapter-wise PYQs ↗

The 3 questions in this mini-test are Rankers Nest original practice questions, not verbatim historical PYQs. Use the source link for the chapter-wise historical question collection.

Q1. A constant force of 10 N moves an object 3 m in its direction. Work done is:

Answer: D. 30 J

W = Fs cos0° = 10×3 = 30 J.

Q2. A 2 kg body initially at rest receives 16 J net work. Its final speed is:

Answer: B. 4 m/s

W = ΔK = ½mv², so 16 = v² and v=4 m/s.

Q3. A machine does 600 J of work in 20 s. Average power is:

Answer: B. 30 W

P = W/t = 600/20 = 30 W.

Conservation of Momentum

Impulse, recoil and system momentum

Q1. A 2 kg body moves at 3 m/s. Its linear momentum is:

Answer: C. 6 kg m/s

p=mv=2×3=6 kg m/s.

Q2. A 0.2 kg ball changes velocity from +10 m/s to −5 m/s. Impulse on it is:

Answer: C. 3 Ns

J=Δp=m(v-u)=0.2(-5-10)=-3 Ns; magnitude is 3 Ns.

Q3. Two bodies stick together after collision. The collision is:

Answer: B. perfectly inelastic

When they stick together, kinetic-energy loss is maximum for the given momentum; collision is perfectly inelastic.

Q1. The momentum of a 4 kg object moving at 5 m/s is:

Answer: B. 20 kg m/s

p=mv=4×5=20 kg m/s.

Q2. A force of 6 N acts for 0.5 s. Impulse magnitude is:

Answer: B. 3 Ns

For constant force, J=FΔt=6×0.5=3 Ns.

Q3. The area under a force-time graph gives:

Answer: C. impulse

Impulse is the time integral of force, equal to the area under an F–t graph.

CHAPTER 08

Collision

Elastic and inelastic collisions, restitution

Browse chapter-wise PYQs ↗

The 3 questions in this mini-test are Rankers Nest original practice questions, not verbatim historical PYQs. Use the source link for the chapter-wise historical question collection.

Q1. In a perfectly elastic collision, which quantities are conserved for an isolated system?

Answer: C. Both momentum and kinetic energy

For an isolated system, momentum is conserved; perfect elasticity also conserves total kinetic energy.

Q2. A 1 kg body moving at 4 m/s collides head-on elastically with an identical body at rest. After collision, the first body's speed is:

Answer: A. 0 m/s

For a one-dimensional elastic collision of equal masses, velocities are exchanged.

Q3. The coefficient of restitution e for a perfectly inelastic collision is:

Answer: A. 0

The bodies have zero relative speed after sticking, so e=0.

CHAPTER 09

Gravitation

Field, potential, satellites and escape velocity

Browse chapter-wise PYQs ↗

The 3 questions in this mini-test are Rankers Nest original practice questions, not verbatim historical PYQs. Use the source link for the chapter-wise historical question collection.

Q1. If the distance between two point masses is doubled, gravitational force becomes:

Answer: C. F/4

F∝1/r², so doubling r reduces force to F/4.

Q2. The escape speed from a planet is v_e. At the same point, circular orbital speed is:

Answer: B. v_e/√2

v_e=√(2GM/R), while v_orbit=√(GM/R)=v_e/√2.

Q3. The gravitational potential energy of two masses m and M separated by r, taking zero at infinity, is:

Answer: B. −GMm/r

U = −GMm/r for point masses with zero potential energy at infinity.

CHAPTER 10

Rotation

Torque, moment of inertia and rolling motion

Browse chapter-wise PYQs ↗

The 3 questions in this mini-test are Rankers Nest original practice questions, not verbatim historical PYQs. Use the source link for the chapter-wise historical question collection.

Q1. A force of 10 N acts perpendicular to a lever arm of 0.5 m. Torque magnitude is:

Answer: B. 5 Nm

τ=rF sin90°=0.5×10=5 Nm.

Q2. The moment of inertia of a point mass m at distance r from an axis is:

Answer: B. mr²

For a point mass, I=mr².

Q3. For pure rolling without slipping, the relation between centre speed v and angular speed ω is:

Answer: B. v=ωR

The no-slip rolling condition is v_cm=ωR.

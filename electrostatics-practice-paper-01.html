Formula recap

Coulomb's law: F = k|q₁q₂|/r²

Point-charge field magnitude: E = k|q|/r²

Point-charge potential: V = kq/r (signed q)

Potential energy: U = qV

Remember: electric field is a vector and superposes vectorially; electric potential is a scalar and adds algebraically.

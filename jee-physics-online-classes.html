What to practise for JEE Physics

Mechanics

Build a foundation in kinematics, Newton’s laws, work-energy, momentum, rotation and oscillations. Draw diagrams and define signs and axes before calculating.

Electricity and magnetism

Connect fields, potential, circuits and magnetic forces to their physical meaning; practise both direct applications and multi-step questions.

Timed problem-solving

Practise choosing an efficient method while checking units, limiting cases and whether the answer is physically reasonable.

Review your errors

Classify mistakes as conceptual, mathematical, reading-related or time-management errors, then revisit the topic before repeating similar questions.
